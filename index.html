What is a
Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize shopping links, item names, sizes, prices and comparison notes. It helps you revisit potential purchases and identify missing information. A saved link is a starting point: confirm the current listing, seller details and delivery options before ordering.

03 / From inspiration to information

A Kakobuy shopping guide for the details that matter.

Useful Kakobuy product links need context. Keep enough information beside each link to explain why it is on your list and what still needs checking.

  1. Choose a role.

    Decide whether the piece fills a gap, replaces something worn out or adds a new styling option.

  2. Compare like with like.

    Use the same measurements, currency and cost assumptions for each product comparison.

  3. Recheck before ordering.

    Reopen the listing, confirm the selected variation and save the date of your last check.

What to record beside each product link
Field Record this Why it helps
Identity Item name, original URL, seller details and selected color. Keeps similar-looking listings and variants separate.
Fit Size chart, garment measurements and comparison with an item you own. Makes sizing decisions more specific than S, M or L.
Materials Material composition, lining and care instructions from the listing. Highlights differences that photographs may not reveal.
Evidence Available quality control photos, measurement images and unresolved questions. Separates observed details from seller claims.
Cost Item price, currency, shipping estimate, fees and possible import charges. Creates a more complete shopping budget.
Conditions Return policy, delivery restrictions and date last checked. Prevents old notes from being mistaken for current terms.

This is a planning checklist, not a live product feed. Photographs alone cannot establish authenticity, exact fit or long-term durability.

05 / Questions before checkout

Clear answers.
Better decisions.

Keep these distinctions in mind when browsing a spreadsheet or following a saved product link.

Is this an official Kakobuy website?

No. This is an independent category and shopping guide that links to CAFANS. It is not operated by Kakobuy or the brands pictured, and it does not provide Kakobuy account services.

How do I use a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

Choose a category, open a relevant listing and record its URL, variation, measurements and price. Compare the available information with alternatives, estimate the full cost, then confirm the current listing and purchase conditions before ordering.

Where do the category and image links go?

They open the relevant category on CAFANS. General browsing buttons open the CAFANS homepage. The brand photographs are style references; their links do not promise an exact product match.

Can every saved link be purchased through Kakobuy?

Do not assume compatibility. Confirm the current platform rules, seller link support, product restrictions and delivery availability directly with the service you intend to use. This page does not test those conditions.

What should I check in quality control photos?

Compare the selected color and variation, visible stitching, labels, hardware and any requested measurements. Ask about unclear details. Photos can show visible condition, but they cannot by themselves prove authenticity, fit or durability.

Why can the final cost differ from the spreadsheet?

Saved prices and estimates can become outdated. The selected variation, shipping method, package size, service fees, currency conversion and applicable import charges may change the final amount. Recheck all quoted costs before payment.

What makes a useful clothing or shoe spreadsheet?

A useful list records accurate links, category, variation, relevant measurements, cost assumptions and a last-checked date. Keep unknown details marked as unknown, and remove entries that no longer fit your needs.
